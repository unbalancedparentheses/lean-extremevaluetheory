# Extreme Value Theory in Lean

A machine-checked library of extreme value theory and heavy-tailed probability in Lean 4 and
Mathlib: limits of maxima, domains of attraction, regular variation, peaks over threshold, stable
laws, the statistics of extremes, extremes of dependent sequences, multivariate extremes, point
processes and the Skorokhod space. Every proof is checked by Lean's kernel and re-checked by an
independent verified kernel checker; every statement has been compared with its textbook source.
